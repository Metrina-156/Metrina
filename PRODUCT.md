# Freelance Web Developer Portfolio

## Users
High-end clients, creative agencies, and startups looking for bespoke, technically sophisticated web experiences. They value craft, precision, and unique digital identities over generic solutions.

## Brand
Elite, minimal, and architecturally precise. The brand feels like a high-end design studio—warm but disciplined. It avoids the "tech-bro" aesthetic in favor of a "digital artisan" persona.

## Tone
Confident, understated, and professional. The language is direct and lacks fluff. It speaks to technical mastery without being loud.

## Anti-References
- Generic SaaS landing pages
- Purple gradients and "AI-slop" visuals
- Standard 3-column feature grids
- Stock illustrations or 3D "claymorphism"
- Oversaturated, neon color palettes

## Strategic Principles
- **Digital Craft:** Every interaction must feel intentional and smooth.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Use white space as a structural element, not just a gap.
- **Technical Sophistication:** Showcase expertise through advanced techniques (Three.js, GSAP, CSS 3D) used with restraint.
