# Product: METRINA Portfolio

## Users
High-end tech startups, elite individual creators, and premium boutique brands looking for an unconventional, high-performance digital presence.

## Brand Tone
Elite, architectural, unconventional, high-performance. Minimalist but impactful. 'Pro Max' aesthetics.

## Strategic Principles
1. **Architectural Composition**: Grid-based, but with creative tension.
2. **Elite Performance**: Snappy, intentional micro-interactions.
3. **Unconventional Luxury**: Eschew generic SaaS clichés (Inter font, purple gradients, cookie-cutter cards).
4. **Honest Materials**: Use noise, grain, and high-contrast typography.
